\chapter{Specifying Human Experience in Interactive Indoor Environments}\label{ch:study-one}

\begin{synopsis}
\synopsislead{What forms of human experience}{are recognised and designed for in research on interactive indoor environments? Research in this area spans HCI, architecture, building science, environmental psychology, and related fields, each contributing different concepts, priorities, and methods for understanding experience. Mapping this interdisciplinary landscape is essential
for clarifying the experiential concerns represented across the field, the design approaches through which they have been addressed, and the directions that require further investigation.}

This chapter develops this account through a scoping review of 192 papers published between 1996 and 2024 and retrieved from the ACM Digital Library and Scopus. Using a hybrid deductive--inductive coding approach, the review identifies 11 targeted human experiences, 20 design mechanisms, and two typologies of technological intervention.

Within the trajectory of the dissertation, this review provides the broad conceptual foundation from which the subsequent studies develop. It addresses \rqref{scoping} by specifying which forms of human experience have been pursued in research on interactive indoor environments and how they have been approached through design. Emotional experience is found to be a substantial strand of this landscape, while its potential as a methodological entry point for investigating situated indoor experience remains underdeveloped. This finding motivates the dissertation's first narrowing of focus, from mapping the breadth of human experience addressed across the field to examining what an affective lens can reveal about its situated complexity.
\end{synopsis}

\begin{chapterclosing}{Concluding synthesis}

The review specifies the forms of human experience currently addressed across research on interactive indoor environments, together with the design mechanisms and technological interventions through which they have been pursued.

Emotional experience emerges as a substantial strand of this
research landscape and as a promising direction for further
investigation. Within the dissertation, this finding motivates the
use of emotion and Affective Interaction as a methodological entry
point for examining how spatial, environmental, technological, and
social conditions acquire significance within particular situations.

\chaptercarryforward{The following chapter develops this direction through Affective Interaction, moving from a field-level mapping of human experience to a situated investigation of how indoor experience is sensed, interpreted, anticipated, and made meaningful.}

\end{chapterclosing}
