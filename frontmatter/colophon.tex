This dissertation was typeset using \LaTeX{}, with a mixture of \emph{classicthesis}\sidenote{\url{https://bitbucket.org/amiede/classicthesis/wiki/Home}} and \emph{tufte-latex}\sidenote{\url{https://tufte-latex.github.io/tufte-latex/}}. The design is inspired by Edward Tufte’s approach to visual thinking, analytical typography, and the careful relation between text, margin, and figure. The main text is set in \emph{Minion}, designed by Robert Slimbach for Adobe Originals.\sidenote{\url{https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/minion}}
